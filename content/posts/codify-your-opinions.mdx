## Write it down

Codify your opinions as much as you can: lint rules, style guides, strategies. They become a living history of how you think, write, build, and work — and the rules that keep quality high when agents start authoring alongside you.

> A design language is a vibe — a point of view on the foundations.

## The three layers

A design language is a point of view on typography, color, space, and motion. The platform applies it to components within its limits. Each implementation adds its own constraints.

## Humans hold the gates

Agents can draft. A person approves at the gates that matter — nothing ships without review.

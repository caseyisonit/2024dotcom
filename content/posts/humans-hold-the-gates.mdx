## Draft, then decide

Draft sample. Agents are fast at the first pass: scaffolds, refactors, test cases, first drafts. The gates that matter stay human: what ships, what it promises, and who it might leave out.

## Where the gates go

Put a person at every point where a change becomes a promise to someone else: a merge, a release, a public post.

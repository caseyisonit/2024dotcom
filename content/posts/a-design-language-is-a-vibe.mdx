## A point of view

Draft sample. A design language is a point of view on the foundations: type, color, space, and motion. It becomes core tokens.

## The platform

The platform applies that language to components within its limits. It becomes semantic tokens.

## The implementation

Each implementation adds its own constraints: web components, React, SwiftUI, Figma. It becomes component tokens.
